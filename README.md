# MATP45 - Specialised Course in Linear Functional Analysis

MATP45 Specialised Course in Linear Functional Analysis is an optional second-cycle mathematics course. It focuses on applications of the abstract principles of functional analysis to a broad range of problems in mathematical analysis.

The course runs alongside [MATP35 Linear Functional Analysis](https://github.com/gilbertbrandow/MATP35). MATP35 develops the underlying abstract theory, while MATP45 emphasizes examples, applications, proofs, and problem solving.

## Course content

The detailed programme includes:

- Problems in topology, metrizability, product spaces, and completion of metric spaces
- Normed spaces, equivalent norms, quotient spaces, and completeness
- Hilbert spaces, orthonormal bases, and extension of bounded linear functionals
- Weak convergence and weak topologies
- Applications involving the Schur test and integral operators
- Bounded linear operators, closed range, and adjoint operators
- Spectra of integral and multiplication operators
- Compact operators and applications of spectral theory

Section and page references in the course programme refer to the course textbook unless otherwise stated.

## Assessment

The course is assessed through:

- Oral presentations of solutions to problems or proofs of relevant results during the course
- A problem-solving project at the end of the course

The detailed programme also refers to preparation for a take-home examination on 14 December. The available information does not clarify whether the take-home examination is the problem-solving project described in the course overview.

A presentation of a textbook section is worth 3 of the 5 examination points available for a presentation. Students who want to present a section should notify the lecturer in good time.

The available material does not yet specify presentation assignments, the format and deadline of the final project, or the release and submission dates of the take-home examination.
